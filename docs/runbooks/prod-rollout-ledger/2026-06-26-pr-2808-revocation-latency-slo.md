Adds the `RevocationDeliveryLatency` histogram (NHP_REV emit -> AC NHP_RVA ack), a p99 < 15s SLO alarm, a companion `RevocationAgedOut` non-delivery alarm, and a `RevocationUntrackable` invariant-break alarm. All alarms key on `{Environment, Cell}` and are additive. At the time of PR #2808 the sample/ack data existed only once the revocation retry engine was armed; issue #2793's activation ledger now tracks that fleet flip. After the per-slot proof fix (#2793), histogram samples are per targeted AC slot, not per `acId` or per revoke event; blue/green overlap can increase sample volume/weighting and aged-out counts, so read p99 as the per-slot delivery SLO.
