Vendored web fonts for the LayerV status page.

These are the same font families the layerv.ai website loads via next/font.
They are vendored here (instead of loaded from Google Fonts) so the status
page has zero third-party runtime dependencies and keeps its CSP at
default-src 'self'.

  https://openfontlicense.org
